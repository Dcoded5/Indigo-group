# EASY MOVEMENT

**A campus tricycle (keke) booking app for Babcock University**

**Team:** The Mentalist
**Members:** Victor, Peter, Ebenezer, Ebuka, Danny
**Date:** 21st November 2026

---

## 1. Problem

Students struggle to find a keke on campus, especially after lectures and at busy times. They waste time waiting or searching, and sometimes arrive late. Unclear fares, cash problems, and poor communication with drivers make it worse.

---

## 2. Who Is It For?

### Student (main user)
Any Babcock student who needs to move around campus, whether to lectures, the hostel, the cafeteria, or the library.

- **What they want:** A quick and reliable ride, without having to search for one.
- **What frustrates them:** Waiting a long time, not knowing where to find a keke, not knowing the fare before they ride, and problems with cash or change.

### Driver (keke operator)
The keke operators who carry students around campus.

- **What they want:** More passengers, ride requests that are clear and organized, and fair, easy payment.
- **What frustrates them:** Driving around looking for passengers, arguments over payment, and not being able to communicate clearly with students.

### Admin (transport manager)
The person in charge of campus transport.

- **What they want:** To manage drivers, set fares, and see reports on how the service is doing.
- **What frustrates them:** Not being able to monitor drivers easily, handling complaints, and keeping the whole service organized.

## 5. Our Solution

A simple mobile app where students can see available kekes, book a ride, see the fare, and pay in the app. Drivers get ride requests on a simple dashboard. Admins manage drivers and fares.

---

## 6. Features

**Students can:**
- See available kekes
- Book a ride (choose pickup and destination)
- See the fare before booking
- Pay in the app
- Track their keke

**Drivers can:**
- Accept ride requests
- Update their availability

**Admins can:**
- Manage drivers, fares, and bookings

---

## 8. How We Will Know It's Working

Easy Movement is a success if:

- Students wait much less for a keke (from [X] minutes down to under [Y] minutes)
- Lots of students use the app to book rides, at least [N]% of all keke trips in the first month
- Students complain less about campus transport, with [N]% fewer complaints
- Drivers actually use it, with [N]% of drivers active on the app
- Students pay through the app instead of cash, for [N]% of rides

---

## 9. What Could Go Wrong

Every new idea has some risks. Here are ours, and what we will do about each one.

- **Drivers may not want to use the app.** We will keep it very simple and involve drivers from the start.
- **Campus internet can be slow.** We will make the app light so it works with little data.
- **Some students prefer cash.** We will make paying in the app easy and safe so they feel comfortable.
- **GPS may not always be accurate.** Students can pick from fixed pickup points or set their location manually.
- **Drivers may not always be available.** Drivers can update their status in the app so students always know who is free.

**What we are assuming:**
- Students and drivers have smartphones.
- The university allows this system.
